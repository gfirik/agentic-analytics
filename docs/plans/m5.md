# M5 plan: Analysis library

Branch: `m5/analysis-library`. Human gate: `no`.
Sources: docs/ROADMAP.md M5, docs/specs/analysis-library.md (all sections), report-schema §3 (Fact), DECISIONS D9.

## Shape

`packages/analysis` has two layers:

1. **Pure deterministic math** (no I/O; every function has hand-computed tests with the arithmetic in comments). It covers statistics, baselines and noise, the insight templates, the decomposers, impact ranges and ranking.
2. **A runner** that reads series through the M4 metric compiler (SQL in DuckDB), applies the math, and records every number as a fact. Each fact carries a computation record (`sql` with params, or `function` with input fact ids) and citations to input rows. The runner writes the `facts`, `insights` and `decompositions` tables.

Facts are the only place numbers live (report-schema §3). The runner hands out the ids (`N1…`), and every insight or decomposition references facts by id.

## Tasks (in order, test-first)

1. **`stats`:** median, MAD, mean, robust z, Theil–Sen slope, Mann–Kendall S/variance (tie-corrected)/z/p, normal CDF (erf), two-proportion z-test, HHI.
2. **Baselines and noise (§2).**
   - Baseline choice: same period last year (needs ≥ 13 months) → previous period → trailing mean of 3. The method is recorded.
   - The MAD band comes from period-over-period changes.
   - Materiality needs all three: |Δ| > 2 × 1.4826 × MAD, |Δ%| ≥ 5% (configurable), and |Δ| ≥ 1% of the series mean.
   - With fewer than 6 periods there is no MAD band and confidence is capped at medium.
   - Minimum-data skips (trend 4, anomaly 6, YoY 13, break 3 + 3) each produce an open-question note.
3. **Insight templates (§3).** Each returns an insight draft with its numbers as fact drafts.
   - `period_change.v1` (latest vs baseline, per metric and per dimension member)
   - `trend.v1` (Theil–Sen + Mann–Kendall, p < 0.05)
   - `trend_break.v1` (best single split minimizing SSE, ≥ 3 per side, mean difference > noise band; reports the change across the break)
   - `anomaly.v1` (robust z of the residual vs baseline > 3.5)
   - `top_contributors.v1`
   - `concentration.v1` (top-1 ≥ 30% or HHI ≥ 0.25)
   - `new_lost.v1`
   - `mix_shift.v1` (≥ 3 pp)
   - `ratio_shift.v1` (two-proportion z when counts are known, else the noise band)
   - `benchmark_gap.v1` (external range in, always labelled external)
4. **Decomposers (§4):**
   - `contribution.v1`: additive; shares can exceed 100%; positive and negative contributors are reported separately.
   - `pvm.v1`: volume, mix and price effects plus new/lost items. The identity is exact, checked by a property test over ≥ 20 randomized cases.
   - `ratemix.v1`: mix and rate effects, plus the Simpson guard (`simpson_risk` when the overall rate moves against most segment rates).
   - `diff.v1`: support and risk ratio over 1–2 attribute combinations; support ≥ 5%, RR ≥ 2, top 5 by support × log(RR).
   - `shapley.v1` is P1: not built (recorded).
5. **`impact.v1` (§5):** scenarios from an allowed set. Each parameter range is validated, the low/high are computed net of the action's cost, and the result is labelled "scenario estimate" with its formula and inputs.
   - `recover_share` 0.3–0.9
   - `price_change` −15%…+15%
   - `remove_excess_rate`
   - `retain_at_risk` 0.3–0.9
6. **Ranking (§6):** |impact on the objective metric / that metric's period total| × confidence weight (high 1, medium 0.6, low 0.3) × recency weight (0.8 per period back). The top 3–7 go to EXPLAIN.
7. **Runner `analyzeJob(ws, defs, catalog, options)`:**
   - Measure: each metric per period, and per dimension member (top 50 + "other").
   - Run the templates on each series.
   - Decompose material changes over each dimension, plus PVM when the metric's table has an item dimension and a quantity column.
   - For ratio metrics, run ratemix over dimensions.
   - Rank the results, then write `facts`, `insights` and `decompositions`.
   - Fact citations name the source table and the `_row` numbers of the input rows. For aggregate facts the cited rows are bounded: every row when there are at most 50, otherwise the first 50 plus a count.
8. **Demo:** `bun run analyze <paths…> [--today] [--currency]` runs ingest → profile → metrics (fallback, `--yes`) → analysis, and prints the ranked insights with their facts.
9. Ledger and README.

## Acceptance → proof

| Criterion | Proof |
|---|---|
| Every method has hand-computed tests | `packages/analysis/test/*` — one describe per method, with the arithmetic in comments |
| PVM identity property test | 25 seeded random cases: volume + mix + price + new + lost = ΔR, exactly up to float tolerance |
| The Simpson fixture triggers the guard | the classic two-segment fixture: every segment rate rises while the overall rate falls → `simpson_risk` |
| Coffee golden outputs exact: −17.9%, 108%, 1.2% → 3.4% | runner on coffee-roaster: revenue trend break at Apr, change Apr vs Mar = −17.913…% (shown −17.9%), wholesale share 107.59…% (shown 108%), refund rate 1.2 (Mar) → 3.4 (Jun) |

## Risks

- **Template thresholds on 6 monthly points:** with fewer than 6 periods there's no MAD band (spec), and exactly 6 periods gives a band from 5 changes. Tests pin each threshold.
- **Mann–Kendall on short series:** use the normal approximation with tie correction, and record that exact small-n tables are not used.
- **Fact volume:** per-member measures can be large. Facts are only created for numbers referenced by insights or decompositions, not for every measured cell.
