agentic-analytics reads a small or medium business's own files — spreadsheets, CSV exports, PDFs, Word documents, emails — and writes a report: **what changed, why it changed, where the data is wrong, and what to do next**.

It is built around three refusals:

1. **The model never does arithmetic.** Every number is a *fact*: the result of SQL run in DuckDB, or of a deterministic function, with its computation attached. Model-written text holds numbers only as `{{N12}}` placeholders that resolve to facts.
2. **No claim without a source.** Every claim carries citations — the rows, cells, pages or URLs it rests on — and a causal grade: observed, correlated, document-supported.
3. **Nothing unverified ships.** A verifier recomputes every number, re-derives the headline metrics another way, checks every citation and every causal word, and drops or downgrades what fails before `report.json` is written.

## What it is not

- Not a dashboard or a BI tool: it answers "what changed and why", once per period.
- Not a chatbot over your files: follow-up questions are answered with verified claims only (`aa ask`).
- Not a forecaster: v0.1 explains the past and proposes checks; it does not predict.
